preset: the attack
  private=1.4.0  public=99.0.0  mode=merged (--extra-index-url)
  verdict  : installs acme-internal-utils 99.0.0 from PUBLIC — confused   [verdict bad]
  explain  : --extra-index-url merges both registries into one pool, and the resolver's only rule is “highest version wins” — it has no concept of which source is trusted. 99.0.0 > 1.4.0, so the attacker's package installs and its setup.py runs as you.

  verdict  : installs acme-internal-utils 1.4.0 — safe   [verdict ok]
  explain  : --index-url means exactly one index is configured. The resolver has no second registry to compare against, so the public look-alike is never even fetched, let alone considered — its version number doesn't matter here at all.

preset: “win the race” (unreliable)
